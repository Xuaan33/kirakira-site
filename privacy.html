Kira Kira

Privacy policy

Effective 25 September 2026

Kira Kira is a free price-comparison app for shoppers in Malaysia, made by an independent developer. This page explains what the app does with information. In short: there's no account, no ads and no tracking, and your photos never leave your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Price tags you scan. The camera picture is read on your phone by Google's ML Kit text recognition. It isn't saved or uploaded.
  • Your comparisons and settings, such as saved comparisons, language and theme, are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

What leaves your phone

  • Online searches. When you search for a product online, the words you type go to Kira Kira's price server, which asks shopping platforms such as Shopee and Lazada for current prices. The app sends nothing that identifies you: no name, account, device ID or location. The server is hosted by Supabase and may run outside Malaysia. Like any web service, it keeps short-lived technical logs, which can include your internet (IP) address, to keep the service running and secure.
  • Text recognition. ML Kit sends Google measurements of how it performs and is used, never the picture, and may download updates to its models.
  • Opening a seller. When you tap a seller, their page opens in the platform's app or your browser through an affiliate link, which tells the platform the visit came from Kira Kira. From there, that platform's own privacy policy applies.

What we don't do

No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools. We don't sell or share personal information.

Permissions

  • Camera, to read price tags. You can type a price in instead.
  • Internet, for online prices and the text recognition described above.

Children

Kira Kira isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.
