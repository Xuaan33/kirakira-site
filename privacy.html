Kira Kira

Privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Kira Kira is a free app for shoppers in Malaysia, made by an independent developer: it compares prices, and works out your month and your pay. This page explains what the app does with information. In short: there are no ads and no tracking, an account is optional, your photos never leave your phone, and your money details leave it only if you choose to back them up to an account, or to share the bills you pay together with your partner.

What stays on your phone

  • Price tags and receipts you scan. The camera picture is read on your phone by Google's ML Kit text recognition. It isn't saved or uploaded. Of a receipt, only what you record from it is kept, as any record is: the shop's name, the day, the amount and what it was for. Anything else printed on it, such as a member's name or number, is read only to find those and isn't kept.
  • Files you share to Kira. When you share a PDF or a picture to Kira Kira from another app, such as an e-bill or a bank transfer screenshot, it's read on your phone: by ML Kit on Android, and by Apple's own text recognition on iPhone. Kira reads a copy, which is deleted once the share card closes; nothing is uploaded. As with a receipt, only what you record from it is kept.
  • Your location, if you allow it. On the phone, the app uses it to find your district for shop prices, the wet markets near you for the pasar price check, and, when you scan, the shop you're in, so the price tags you scan are marked with it. The location itself is never sent anywhere; the app keeps only the district it found, and the shops you've been to.
  • Your month, if you set it up: what the app calls you; the month and year you were born; whether you're married and whether your spouse has an income; how many children you have; your salary and pay day; the day your month starts; whether you pay LINDUNG 24 Jam; what you pay every month, with amounts and due days; your savings and investments (what each one is, such as a fixed deposit or ASB, what you put aside each month, and a fixed deposit's rate, term and start) and what you tell the app about them: what you put in or took out, what they were worth, what they paid, and your EPF balance; what you record spending or receiving, with its amount, what it was for, the day and any note; the monthly payments you set aside for a month; and when to remind you, and about what. The app uses them on your phone to work out your take-home pay (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, LINDUNG 24 Jam and the monthly tax deduction), where your money went, what's left each month, and what your savings are worth and made. They stay on your phone, unless you sign in to back them up (see Your account). You can change them in the app, and deleting the app deletes them all. The app never asks for a bank login, and isn't linked to any bank or investment account: it knows only what you tell it.
  • Reminders. On the days that apply, the app asks whether your pay came in, whether a payment due was paid, what a bill that changes came to, and whether this month's savings went in; now and then, what a saving is worth or paid, your EPF balance once a year, and what you did when a fixed deposit's term ended. Its notifications are planned and shown by your phone itself: nothing is sent to plan them, and no server sends them. A question you put off until tomorrow is noted on this phone only.
  • Your comparisons, prices and settings, such as saved comparisons; your prices (each price you add to a comparison, with its shop and the day), so the app can tell you where you saw something for less; the shops you've been to (their names, and where your phone was when you picked them); language, theme and your district for shop prices. They are stored on your phone, and backed up to your account if you sign in. You can delete your prices in the app. Deleting the app deletes them all from the phone.

Your account (optional)

Kira Kira works the same without an account. If you sign in with Google or Apple, the app keeps a copy of your things in your account, so they come back if you change phones:

  • What Kira gets from Google or Apple: your name and email address, nothing else. Apple may give a private relay address instead of your own.
  • What your account keeps: your month as set up, what you record, the payments you set aside, your savings and what you told the app about them, your saved comparisons, your prices and the names of the shops you've been to, as described above, each kept as you make or change it. Where your phone was when you picked a shop stays on your phone; your phone's location is never sent.
  • Where: on Kira Kira's server, hosted by Supabase, in Australia (Sydney). Your account's data can be read only by you, signed in on your phone. It travels encrypted and is encrypted where it's stored.
  • Backups: every night, a copy of every account is encrypted with a key only the developer holds and kept for 14 days, privately, on GitHub, so accounts can be restored if the server fails.
  • Your choices: in Me, Download my data gives you a file of everything the app keeps. Sign out takes your things off the phone; they stay in your account. Delete account and data deletes your account and everything in it from the server straight away; copies in the nightly backups are gone within 14 days. What's on the phone stays until you delete the app.

Sharing with a partner (optional)

With an account, you can share a household with one partner: one of you sends an invite code, and the other types it in. A code works once, within 7 days, and is deleted once used.

  • What your partner sees: the name the app calls you; the monthly payments you choose to pay together, with their amounts, due days and who pays each; whether and when each was paid, and by whom; what either of you records for the home, with its amount, what it was for, the day, any note and who paid; and the joint payments set aside for a month. Your take-home pay for the month is shown to your partner only while you turn on Show my income, and never how it's worked out.
  • What stays yours: your salary and everything about your pay, your own records, payments and savings, and anything you don't mark for the household.
  • Where: what the household shares is kept on Kira Kira's server, where your account is (see Your account), and can be read only by the two of you, signed in. It is in the nightly backups too.
  • Leaving: in Me, Leave household takes the household off your phone. What you shared stays with your partner, under "a former member" instead of your name; the payments you pay, and what you paid, stay with you as your own. Deleting your account does the same. When the last of you leaves or deletes their account, the household and everything it shares are deleted from the server straight away; copies in the nightly backups are gone within 14 days.

What leaves your phone

  • Searches. When you search for a product, the words you type go to Kira Kira's price server. For prices in shops, your state and district go with them (found from your location, or picked by you), so that shops in your district come first. To tell which shop you're in, the app downloads the list of shops in your district (and any next to it) from the same server; only the state and district go with that request, and the nearest shop is worked out on your phone. The shop list comes from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). For the pasar price check, the app downloads the list of wet markets, works out on your phone which are near you, and asks the server for those markets' prices; like your district, which markets it asks about says roughly where you are. Searches send nothing that identifies you: no name, account or device ID, and never your phone's location. The server is hosted by Supabase and may run outside Malaysia. Like any web service, it keeps short-lived technical logs, which can include your internet (IP) address, to keep the service running and secure.
  • Text recognition. ML Kit sends Google measurements of how it performs and is used, never the picture, and may download updates to its models.
  • Searching an online store. When you tap Shopee or Lazada under a search, that store's own search opens with your words, in its app or your browser. For Lazada, the words first go to Kira Kira's server, which asks Lazada for an affiliate link to that search; the link tells Lazada the visit came from Kira Kira. From there, the store's own privacy policy applies.
  • Finding a shop. When you tap a shop, Google Maps opens with its name and address, and Google's privacy policy applies there.
  • Your account, if you make one, as described above. Signing in goes through Google's or Apple's own sign-in, and their privacy policies apply there.
  • What you share with a partner, if you do, as described above.

What we don't do

No bank logins, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no account unless you want one. We don't sell or share personal information.

Permissions

  • Camera, to read price tags. You can type a price in instead.
  • Location, to find your district for shop prices and the wet markets near you (approximate is enough) and the shop you're in when you scan (precise). You can pick your area and type the shop instead.
  • Notifications, to remind you on the day: pay day, payments due and the bills that change. You can turn each kind off in the app, or all of them in your phone's settings; the app then asks only when you open it. On Android, the app also plans its reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet, for shop prices, the online stores' search, and the text recognition described above.

Children

Kira Kira isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
