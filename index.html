Kira Kira is a free price-comparison app for shoppers in Malaysia, on the shelf and online.

Coming soon to Google Play

On the shelf

Point your camera at the price tags. Kira Kira reads each price and size, works out what you pay per 100 ml, per kg or per piece, and tells you which is cheaper, even when a deal like “2 untuk RM65” isn't. Tags are read on your phone; photos never leave it.

Online

Search for a phone or gadget and see its price on each platform, such as Shopee, Lazada and the brand's own store, with the cheapest official sellers by name. Tap one to open it in the platform's app.

In your language

English, Bahasa Melayu and 中文.

How it stays free

No ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy in the app. When you buy through a link in Kira Kira, we may earn a commission from the platform. It never changes your price, or the order the prices are shown in.
