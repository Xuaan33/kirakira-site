( Questions )

Asked before you ask.

Is Kira Kira free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy in the app.

So how does it make money?

When you search an online store from Kira Kira, such as Lazada, and buy there, the store may pay us a commission. It never changes your price.

Does it upload my photos?

No. Tags are read on your phone, and photos are never saved or sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Which price tags can it read?

Printed shelf tags in Malaysian shops, including deals like “2 untuk” and “Beli 2 Percuma 1”, member prices (Harga Ahli), savings (Jimat) and prices per kg. If a tag won't read, type it in.

Where do the shop prices come from?

From PriceCatcher, KPDN's daily record of everyday goods' prices at shops across Malaysia, published as open data on data.gov.my.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and scan.

When can I get it?
